Contact

Need help, found a bug, or want a particular machine added to the workshop? Email info@iraady.com and we will reply.

How to play

  • Pick a build from the workshop. Every build starts as a few big chunks.
  • Pull a part up out of the parts bin and drop it where it belongs on the blueprint.
  • Each stage splits the same object into one more piece than the last — a 28-part e-scooter runs to 28 stages, down to every screw and cable.
  • Later stages hide the part names: identify each piece by its shape.
  • Stuck? Each stage gives you three hints that reveal where a part belongs.
  • Finish a stage without mistakes for three stars. Clear every stage to master a build and watch it do the job it was made for.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login, and the app collects no personal data.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game works completely offline.

How do I reset my progress?

Delete and reinstall the app. Progress is stored only on your device.

Is there any cost, advertising or in-app purchase?

No. The app is free and contains none of these.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no external links in gameplay and no data collection.

Privacy

See the Assembo privacy policy. In short: no data is collected.
